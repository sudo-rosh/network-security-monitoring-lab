# Organization
- **Name:** Helix BioLogix Labs
- **Industry:** Clinical Laboratory and Diagnostic Services
- **Size:** Small organization (~50 employees) working in a 2-storey diagnostic facility.
**What they do**
A private medical testing laboratory that analyzes patient blood and DNA samples to detect genetic diseases and cancer risks for local hospitals.

---
# Purpose of the Network

The network supports the organization's diagnostic, medical-review, administrative, and IT operations.
The network mainly supports:
- Operation of diagnostic instruments and systems.
- Transfer and storage of diagnostic test results.
- Access to patient records.
- Review and modification of medical records by authorized medical staff.
- Billing and administrative activities.
- External access to selected test results through a web portal.
- IT and network administration.
