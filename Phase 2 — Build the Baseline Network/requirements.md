# Types of Users / Devices

## 1. Testing Lab

- **People:** Lab technicians.
- **What they do:** Run patient blood/tissue samples through testing equipment and transfer generated test results to the Patient-Database.
### Devices
- `Server-PT` labeled as **Blood-Analyzer**
    - Represents the diagnostic system that analyzes blood samples and generates test results.
- `Server-PT` labeled as **DNA-Sequencer**
    - Represents the diagnostic system that analyzes DNA samples and generates test results.
- `PC-PT` labeled as **Lab-Tech-PC**
    - Receives results generated by the diagnostic systems.
    - Used by lab technicians to transfer results to the Patient-Database.
**Access:**
- Lab technicians can access the Blood-Analyzer and DNA-Sequencer.
- Lab technicians can use the Lab-Tech-PC.
- Doctors and medical reviewers do not have direct access to the diagnostic systems because they are not trained to operate them.

---
## 2. Doctors & Medical Reviewers
- **People:** Pathologists and lab doctors.
- **What they do:** Review test results, write diagnoses, and sign off on medical reports.
### Devices
- `PC-PT` labeled as **Doctor-PC-01**
- `Laptop-PT` labeled as **Doctor-PC-02**

**Access:**
- Doctors and medical reviewers can access the Patient-Database.
- They can review and modify appropriate patient records.
- They cannot directly access the Blood-Analyzer or DNA-Sequencer.

---
## 3. Front Desk & Billing
- **People:** Receptionists and billing staff.
- **What they do:** Register patients, process insurance payments, and perform billing-related activities.
### Devices

- `PC-PT` labeled as **Reception-PC**
- `PC-PT` labeled as **Billing-PC**
- Shared **Network Printer**
**Access:**
- Billing staff have view-only access to relevant information in the Patient-Database for billing purposes.
- Billing staff cannot modify patient records.
	- Front-desk staff must be able to enter new patient details into the Patient-Database.
- Front-desk staff must not be able to modify diagnostic results or medical records.

---
## 4. IT & Core Systems
- **People:** Network administrator.
- **What they do:**
    - Manage network infrastructure.
    - Administer servers.
    - Configure routers and switches.
    - Verify backups.
    - Maintain system availability.
### Devices
- `PC-PT` labeled as **Admin-Workstation**
- `Server-PT` labeled as **Patient-Database**
    - Stores patient records and diagnostic results.
- `Server-PT` labeled as **Web-Portal**
    - Provides external hospitals with access to selected test results.
**Access:**
- The Admin-Workstation is used for technical administration of network infrastructure and servers.
- IT administrators can technically administer the Patient-Database.
- IT administrators should not modify patient records or diagnostic results as part of their normal administrative role.

# Restricted Resources

The following resources require restricted access:
- **Blood-Analyzer**
    - Accessible only by lab technicians and IT.
- **DNA-Sequencer**
    - Accessible only by lab technicians and IT.
- **Patient-Database**
    - Patient information must not be freely accessible to all users.
    - Doctors/medical reviewers can modify appropriate records.
    - Billing staff have view-only access for billing purposes.
    - IT has technical administrative access but should not modify patient records as part of its normal role.
- **Web-Portal**
    - External hospitals should only have access to information intended for external access.
- **Network infrastructure**
    - Administrative access is restricted to IT.

The **Web-Portal** should not have direct access to the Patient-Database.
The intended data flow is:
```text
Patient-Database
       ↓
   Web-Portal
       ↓
External Hospitals
```

There should be no direct reverse communication:

```text
Web-Portal ──X──> Patient-Database
```

---
# Security Objectives
The project aims to:
- Prevent unauthorized access to internal resources.
- Restrict unnecessary communication between network segments.
- Protect sensitive patient information and diagnostic results.
- Secure administrative access to network infrastructure.
- Apply least-privilege access to sensitive resources.
- Limit the impact of a compromised device.
- Prevent externally accessible systems from providing unnecessary access to internal resources.
- Provide visibility into network traffic for investigation.

---
# Functional Requirements
- Lab technicians must be able to operate the diagnostic systems through the Lab-Tech-PC.
- Blood-Analyzer and DNA-Sequencer systems must be able to generate diagnostic results.
- Lab technicians must be able to transfer generated test results from the Lab-Tech-PC to the Patient-Database.
- Doctors and medical reviewers must be able to retrieve diagnostic results from the Patient-Database.
- Doctors and medical reviewers must be able to associate diagnostic results with appropriate patient records.
- Doctors and medical reviewers must be able to modify appropriate patient records.
- Billing staff must be able to view relevant patient information required for billing.
- Billing staff must not need to modify patient records.
- The Patient-Database must be able to provide selected patient/test information to the Web-Portal.
- The Web-Portal must allow authorized external hospitals to access information intended for them.
- The Web-Portal must not require direct access to the Patient-Database.
- IT staff must be able to technically administer the network infrastructure and servers.
- The network must support communication between systems required for legitimate business operations.
